怎么连上电脑

Pairing with the PC

  1. 电脑上装好 Glance（0.3.0 或更新），打开设置 → 手机遥控，把开关打开。
  2. 点“配对一部手机”里的“用 APP”，电脑上会出现一个二维码。
  3. 手机打开 Glance，扫描这个二维码。手机和电脑要连着同一个路由器（同一个 Wi-Fi 或网线）。
  1. Install Glance (0.3.0 or later) on the PC, open its settings → Phone, and turn the switch on.
  2. Under "Pair a phone", press "For the app": a code appears on the PC.
  3. Open Glance on the phone and scan the code. The phone and the PC must be on the same network (the same router).

遥控只在你家的局域网里工作，不经过任何服务器；每部手机有自己的配对密钥，随时可以在电脑的设置里移除。

The remote works only on your own network and goes through no server; each phone has a key of its own, which you can remove in the PC's settings at any time.
